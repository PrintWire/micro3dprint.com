About Micro 3D Print

Covering additive manufacturing for microfluidics and lab-on-chip additive manufacturing — from concept to production hardware.

OUR STORY

Microfluidics at the Frontier of Fabrication

Micro 3D Print covers additive manufacturing for microfluidics — from lab-on-a-chip and droplet generators to micromixers, microreactors, and organ-on-chip devices.

Our editorial team includes microfluidics and bioengineering specialists with real project experience across chip design, droplet systems, and tissue models.

PURPOSE

Our Mission

To cover microfluidic AM from the single channel to the full lab-on-a-chip with equal depth — the geometry, the seal, and the material behind them.

Our Vision

To be the authoritative, vendor-independent source for additive manufacturing in microfluidics — connecting engineers to the suppliers reshaping how chips get made.

WHAT DRIVES US

What Sets Us Apart

💧 Whole-Chip

From a single channel to a full organ-on-chip — we cover microfluidic AM across the device.

◆ Process-Literate
